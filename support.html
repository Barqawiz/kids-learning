Getting started

Which devices does Phonics House work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. On iPhone the game plays in landscape; on iPad it works in both landscape and portrait. Everything plays offline.

How do I choose or change my child's age mode?

You pick Preschool (ages 3-4) or Early School (ages 5-7) during setup. To change it later, tap the gear button on the home screen, pass the parent gate and choose a mode under Child Learning Profile.

What is the parent gate?

Before buying, restoring purchases, redeeming a code or opening settings, the app shows three numbers written as words (for example "Four · Two · Seven"). Tap the matching digits to continue. It keeps young children from buying or leaving the app by accident.

What is free?

The letters S, A and M are open with all their games, along with the first numbers and colors. Everything else opens with Phonics House Premium.

Purchases and subscriptions

What does Phonics House Premium include?

All six rooms, all 26 letters, every mini-game and story, plus the full Numbers and Colors sections. You can choose a monthly plan, a yearly plan with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime unlock. The app shows each price in your own currency before you pay.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?
  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. Open Phonics House and tap any locked room or letter, or open settings with the gear button.
  3. Pass the parent gate and tap Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel or change a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Phonics House Premium. Subscribers can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I redeem an offer code?

On the purchase screen tap Redeem Code, or open settings and tap Redeem Offer Code, then enter the code in Apple's sheet. You can also redeem a code in the App Store app by tapping your account picture and then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

Can my family share the purchase?

Yes for the lifetime unlock: it works with Apple's Family Sharing, so family members in your Family Sharing group get it too. The monthly and yearly subscriptions are for the Apple Account that bought them.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and play

There is no sound.

Check that the side switch is not on silent and the volume is up. In the app's settings you can turn background music and the voice on or off separately.

A tracing stroke doesn't count.

Start at the green dot and follow the dotted path in one go. If the finger wanders far from the line, the stroke resets so your child can try again.
