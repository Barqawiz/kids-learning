Privacy Policy

Phonics House for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Phonics House has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites. What the app saves about your child stays on your device. The only thing it sends is anonymous usage statistics that carry nothing identifying you or your child, described below.

What is stored on your device

The app stores the following on the device only:

  • Your child's profile: a name if you enter one (optional), boy or girl (used to pick a default name and picture), and the learning mode (ages 3-4 or 5-7).
  • Progress through rooms, letters and games, plus stars and stickers.
  • Settings such as music and voice, and whether the full version or a subscription is active.

We have no server and no account system, and we cannot read any of this. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn what to improve, the app sends anonymous events through Google Analytics for Firebase. Examples: a room was opened, a game was started or finished, the purchase screen was opened and from where, a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan was chosen, a purchase was completed or cancelled. Each event carries only fixed values, such as a room or letter number, a game type, or the learning mode.

The app never sends your child's name, anything typed, location, prices or payment details. We set no user ID, so nothing is tied to you. The app is built without Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), never shows a tracking-permission prompt, and turns off advertising-related data collection before the first event is sent. Like any internet service, Google may infer an approximate region (country or city) from the IP address. Events wait on the device and are sent when a connection is available, so playing never needs the internet.

Ad measurement

If the app was installed after tapping an ad, Apple's privacy-preserving attribution (SKAdNetwork) may tell the advertiser, in aggregate, that an install led to a finished game or a purchase. Apple designs this so that no one, including us, learns who you are.

Purchases and subscriptions

The monthly and yearly subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are made through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed or cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Phonics House is designed for children aged 3 to 7, used with a parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The child's name is optional and never leaves the device. Buying, restoring purchases, redeeming a code, opening settings and opening any outside link are behind a parent gate.

Device permissions

The app asks for one optional permission: notifications, and only if a parent turns on play reminders in settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device. The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Third parties

We use one third party: Google Analytics for Firebase, only for the anonymous statistics described above, with no advertising and no cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share any data. The other services the app uses are Apple's: the App Store and In-App Purchase.

Your choices

Your child's data is on your device, and you can delete it by deleting the app. The statistics are anonymous and cannot be linked to you. You are still welcome to write to us with any question or request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
